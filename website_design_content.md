__Website Design__

Our existing website \([https://raaminfra\.netlify\.app/\#projects](https://raaminfra.netlify.app/#projects)\) is one pager with dark theme and couple of sections on stats\. However, as this is a starting project, we don’t have much stats to showcase\. Hence, I have recommended some content for the 4 sections and couple of website designs to give an idea on how with minimal content we can fill the page\.

Key inputs below:

1. Build a lightweight one\-pager with 4 scroll sections 
	1. Home
	2. Service Capabilities
	3. About Us
	4. Contact Us
2. __Hero Banner__: Clean logo and name \(e\.g\., "RAAM Infrastructure and Developer LLP"\), 
3. __Home__
	1. aviation tagline like "Engineering India's Airport Future"\. 
	2. Subtext: From Residential Master Planning to Airport Development
	3. Add generic airport horizon image
	4. Key Stats to be presented
		1. 5\+ million sq ft residential master plans 
		2. Integrated township planning
		3. 3D isometric renders securing 100% regulatory approvals
		4. Phased land use optimization
4. __Service Capabilities__
	1. Precision \- Design and Planning
	2. Execution Excellence
	3. Strategic \- Financial & Project Structuring
	4. Intelligence driven Tech Integrations
	5. Trusted Stakeholder Relationships
	6. Immersive \- 3D Renders
5. __About US:__
	1. RAAM Infrastructure and Developers LLP specializes in comprehensive real estate development with its promoters having a portfolio of 7\.5\+ million sq ft residential projects across India since 2009\. With proven expertise in master planning to large\-scale project execution, we are strategically expanding into airport infrastructure development\. Our residential success provides the perfect foundation for aviation opportunities\.
	2. Key Stats Highlight:
		1. 17\+ Years – Promoters' industry experience \(2009–2026\)
		2. 7\.5M\+ sq ft – Residential projects delivered
		3. Airport\-Ready – Transferable infrastructure expertise
6. Contact Us:
	1. Input Form; or we can simply say that drop of an email for any queries and mention our contactus email ID\.
7. Footer of the page: LLP address
8. Design Tips

- Use aviation blues/grays, minimal animations, fast\-loading stock images \(runways, terminals\)\. 
- __Framework__: Upgrade to Tailwind CSS or Bootstrap 5 for responsive aviation layouts \(e\.g\., hero with runway imagery\)
- Navbar: Fixed top with logo \(e\.g\., "RaaMinfra Airports"\), links to Home,  Capability, About, Contact\.
- Following are the 2 sites which I refered\. Boomsupersonic one is most aligned with us as it has minimal content and yet it looks impressive\. In images we can have runsways, planes, terminals and other stock images\.
